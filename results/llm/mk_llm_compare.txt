Command: python scenario_macedonian.py --compare-llm --games 20

Weights used: weights_macedonian_debate.npz

This is the first real (non-fallback) --compare-llm measurement for
Macedonian. Key finding: for the first time across all three scenarios,
LLM moderator override actually COST games — 2 losses out of 20 that the
trained-only baseline would have won. This lines up with the visibly
broken Macedonian grammar in mk_llm_demo.txt: the model is producing
Macedonian-shaped text without being reliably correct about it, and here
that unreliability shows up as worse decisions, not just worse sentences.
