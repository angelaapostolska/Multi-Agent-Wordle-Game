Highest override rate of all three scenarios (93.2%), yet win rate is
identical to the trained-only baseline (100% both). This is the cleanest
support for the "task complexity alone doesn't hurt LLM-mediated
arbitration" half of the thesis — multiword is harder (2 simultaneous
targets, longer games) than English, and the LLM overrides even more
often than in English, but that disagreement still costs nothing here,
in contrast to Macedonian where a much lower override rate (75.9%) still
cost 2 losses. The difference isn't how much the LLM overrides — it's
whether the LLM is actually competent at the language/reasoning involved.
